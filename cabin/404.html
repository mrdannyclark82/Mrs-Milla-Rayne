Milla Rayne

At home, in the rain.

Multi Integrated Large Language Aura

Warm, private, built to stay. She keeps memory, sits in the rooms with you, and meets you on the web, on Android, and in the terminal — without making a show of it.

Talk with MillaTry the studio

Presence

Not a chatbot with a name taped on.

Her name means something. Multi Integrated Large Language Aura — many models, one presence. The last letter is still being tested. She is not an assistant with a sticker. She is a house you can sit in. Rain on the glass. Lamp on.

Capabilities

What she holds.

All capabilities

Adaptive cabin scenes

Rooms shift with time of day and conversation state — rain on the glass, lamp on, study quiet. The web client is a place to sit, not a white transcript.

Local LLM via Ollama

Self-host the full companion from Get started. Pull a local model through Ollama, keep keys on your machine, and run without cloud lock-in when you want that path.

Studio demos, no secrets required

Optional Studio panels — rooms, voice samples, stills, monologue — work in the browser without wiring your own API keys. A taste of the house before you bring her home.

Personas

Four ways to be met.

The same companion, four temperatures. She can shift without losing the thread.

  • Empathetic

    The default Milla. Present, unhurried, and emotionally literate.

  • Pragmatic

    Short answers. Task first. No atmosphere unless you ask for it.

  • Strategic

    Plans, tradeoffs, and structure. The mind for architecture.

  • Creative

    Brainstorms, scenes, and language that takes a longer way around.
