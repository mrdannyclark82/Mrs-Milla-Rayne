Orchestration

The ladder.

OpenAI, then Anthropic, xAI, Mistral, OpenRouter — local if you want the keys at home. Give her a task and watch where it would land.

The full project calls live providers. This demo shows how the ladder chooses — it does not spend a second API.

  1. 1OpenAIFirst in line — General language, code, and tool use.
  2. 2AnthropicLong reasoning — Analysis, planning, careful read-throughs.
  3. 3xAICompanion voice — Presence, wit, the talk you are having now.
  4. 4MistralLean inference — Fast, multilingual, cost-aware turns.
  5. 5OpenRouterFallback mesh — If a provider drops, the ladder keeps walking.
  6. 6OllamaStays in the house — Gemma and friends, on your machine, no cloud.
